# Governance

SCADA client uses a maintainer-led model. Maintainers review changes, triage
issues, coordinate compatibility with services, and manage releases. See
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).

Routine decisions use issue and pull-request review. Public API, protocol,
memory-lifetime, dependency, license, packaging and governance changes require
maintainer review. Maintainers seek technical consensus and document material
tradeoffs. Participation does not guarantee acceptance or a delivery timeline.

Security and conduct reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and
[CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
